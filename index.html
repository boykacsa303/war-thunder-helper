War Thunder Helper

Free · Unofficial · Community-made

About the project

War Thunder Helper is a free assistant for vehicle analysis, game mechanics, tactics, and comparisons between in-game performance and real-world vehicles.

What can it help with?

  • Vehicle strengths and weaknesses
  • Firepower, mobility, protection, and survivability
  • Vehicle comparisons and tactical advice
  • Differences between the game and real life
